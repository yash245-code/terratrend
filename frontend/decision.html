    Scientific Integrity

    Why Single Satellites Fail Indian Agriculture.

    Atmospheric sounding, microwave soil sensing, and optical vegetation indices each carry inherent blind spots during the South Asian Summer Monsoon. TerraTrend enforces strict multi-dataset consensus before issuing climate trend advisories.

    Hamed-Rao Serial Correlation

    Standard Mann-Kendall tests flag false trends due to natural multi-year monsoon cycles. The Hamed-Rao modification calculates empirical lag autocorrelations, ensuring p < 0.05 represents true physical climate shifts.

    Zero-Guesswork Farm Economics

    Every cost and yield benchmark references empirical Directorate of Economics and Statistics (DES) and Commission for Agricultural Costs and Prices (CACP) field survey schemes across 70 state-crop combinations.

    Islands & Coastal Land-Surface Fallback

    Small island cells (Lakshadweep, Andaman & Nicobar) marked as water in coarse land models fall back to nearest valid land within 30 km while directly preserving unmasked atmospheric datasets.
